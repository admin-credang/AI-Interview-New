Gaze calibration (about 40 seconds)
1. Keep your meeting window open (do not close or minimise it) and your camera ON.
2. Sit straight, whole face visible, a normal distance from the screen.
3. Press Start. The page goes full screen. Look ONLY at the glowing number and say it aloud. Do not move your head more than needed.
