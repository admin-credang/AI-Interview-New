How automatic Q&A works

  1. Open this page from the AI Interview dashboard (Live Interview Guard), check the candidate details and select the speech language.
  2. Click Share meeting tab + audio. Select the Meet / Zoom / Teams browser tab and enable Share tab audio.
  3. The candidate's face is found automatically - nothing to draw.
  4. Click Start automatic analysis. The page listens to your microphone and the meeting-tab audio, detects who is the interviewer and who is the candidate from who asks the questions, and starts a new question by itself whenever the interviewer asks the next one.
  5. When the interview ends, click End & generate report. The PDF report downloads automatically. No Next Question button is needed.
New in v31.1: only 2. Start automatic analysis and End & generate report are needed. The report now also checks (a) whether each answer is factually correct (offline knowledge bank, or AI key for every question), (b) another voice on the audio, (c) keyboard typing sounds while the question / answer runs (Googling / chat), (d) repeated looks to the same side (phone beside the screen), (e) better reading-pattern detection. Looking down while thinking is no longer a flag.
Automatic speaker detection: the page learns from the first few sentences which audio source asks the questions (interviewer) and which one answers (candidate); if everything arrives on the meeting tab it separates the voices by wording. You can override it above. Use headphones to reduce microphone echo. Chrome must provide a tab-audio track; if not, the page will tell you that candidate audio is unavailable.
Consent & privacy: tell the candidate that the interview audio is being transcribed and get their consent. Chrome's Web Speech recognition may process audio through its speech service; this is not offline transcription. The transcript and report are generated in this browser and are not sent to your own server by this page.

Candidate details

Tip: choose the language the CANDIDATE really speaks. Tamil answer = Tamil (India); English answer = English (India). Wrong language gives garbage words (e.g. "beta science"). Use headphones, speak in full sentences, and keep the candidate near the mic.

Without a key, scores are an offline estimate. With a key, the questions and answers (text only) are sent to the AI provider once, when you click End. The key stays in this page and is never saved.
